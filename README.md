Flying Eyeball Catcher:
Instructions;
You are inside a spooky graveyard underneath a dark red blood moon. All of a sudden, some scary flying eyeball appear. Try to catch as many as you can within the time limit!
Aim;
Within the time limit (20s) you must try to score as many points as you can. Every time a flying eyeball appears on the screen you must quickly click them before they move to a different place. Every time you catch one you gain +10 points. Try to catch as many as you can before time runs out.
How To Play;
Click anywhere on the title screen to start the game.
Try to catch as many eyeballs as you can by moving your mouse over them and clicking.
Watch the timer at the top of the screen (20s) and try to score the most points.
When the time runs out click the game over screen to play again and try to beat your high score!
